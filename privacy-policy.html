Privacy Policy for SuperBuilt
SuperBuilt ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is a workout tracking application developed by an independent developer based in India. We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

When you create an account, we may collect:

  • Name
  • Email address

You may sign in using:

  • Email & Password
  • Google Sign-In

Authentication is provided using Firebase Authentication.

Workout Information

The App stores information that you voluntarily create, including:

  • Workout splits, exercises, sets, repetitions, and weight lifted
  • Workout duration and history
  • Personal Records (PRs) and body weight
  • Training statistics

Device & Analytics Information

When enabled, Firebase services may collect information such as:

  • Device model and operating system
  • App version
  • Crash reports and diagnostic information

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Sync workout data across devices
  • Restore your workout history
  • Improve app performance and fix bugs
  • Provide workout analytics
  • Process subscriptions and purchases
  • Display advertisements in the free version

3. Google Health Connect Data

SuperBuilt may request access to Google Health Connect to read and write your workout data. If you grant this permission, please note our strict policies regarding Health Connect data:

  • We only use Health Connect data to provide core app functionalities (e.g., displaying your workout logs).
  • We do not use Health Connect data for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • We do not sell Health Connect data to third parties.
  • We do not transfer Health Connect data to third parties, except as required to provide the core service or comply with the law.

4. Cloud Services & Advertising

We use Google Firebase services (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Cloud Messaging) which may process data according to Google's Privacy Policy. The free version of the App displays advertisements using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect certain device information and identifiers to display and measure advertisements. You can remove advertisements by purchasing the premium version of the App.

5. Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored locally using a Room Database and securely in Cloud Firestore when synchronization is available. We retain your personal information (such as account details and workout history) for as long as your account is active to provide you with the App's features. System and crash logs are retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes before being automatically deleted.

6. Global Privacy Rights & No "Selling" of Data

SuperBuilt is available globally. Depending on your region (such as the European Economic Area, UK, or California), you may have specific rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data.

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with third-party service providers (such as Google Firebase and AdMob) strictly to operate the App, provide cloud synchronization, and display advertisements.

7. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your information. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

8. Account Deletion

If you wish to request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated cloud data, you can do so by visiting our Account Deletion page at:

superbuilt.com/delete-account.html (Note: update this URL to your actual domain name if needed)

Alternatively, you may contact us at fulgobistudio@gmail.com. We will process deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe.

9. Children's Privacy

SuperBuilt is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove the information.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the Effective Date at the top of this page.
